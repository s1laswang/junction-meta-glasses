# HoldUp: Supervised Pilot and Adoption Plan

Companion runbook for `BRIEF.md` ("A plausible path to adoption or a pilot"), `docs/PLAN.md` (section 0b), and `deck/index.html` (Slide 2, Slide 3, Slide 8, Slide 9, Slide 12, and Slide 13). Status on Sun 15 Nov 2026: **proposed pilot; user outreach in progress** (`docs/USER_TEST_KIT.md`). No signed partnership is claimed beyond committed evidence in this repository.

## 1. Phase 0 Supervised Study (Näkövammaisten liitto / Iiris, Helsinki)

- **Cohort and setting:** Proposed **4-week** supervised home usability and safety study with **20** visually impaired members recruited through **Näkövammaisten liitto** (NKL, Finnish Federation of the Visually Impaired) at the **Iiris** centre in **Helsinki** (Marjaniementie 74; outreach protocol in `docs/USER_TEST_KIT.md`). Participants check their own everyday medicine boxes and food packages alongside their existing safe routines.
- **Honest regulatory positioning:** Checking a pack against a personal medication list can fall under **EU MDR** (Regulation (EU) 2017/745) **Class IIa** software (**Rule 11**), as stated on `deck/index.html` Slide 8 and Slide 9 ("Decision support, not diagnosis."). Consent (`web/src/screens/Onboarding.tsx`) discloses HoldUp as a **research prototype, not CE-marked** ("HoldUp is a research prototype, not a CE-marked medical device"), and Phase 0 runs strictly as a **non-diagnostic assistive information aid and supervised study** with NKL accessibility specialists and community pharmacists before any formal clinical evaluation or CE-marking path.
- **Mandatory pharmacist hand-off:** HoldUp abstains whenever a dose or allergen read falls below threshold. Deterministic gates in `server/src/safety.ts` return **`Not sure`** whenever strength confidence is **`< 0.8`**, product-name confidence is **`< 0.8`**, or an allergen-free read has confidence **`< 0.95`** (or readability **`< 0.8`**), and `server/src/fidelity.ts` checks spoken Live answers against the deterministic sentence. Every `Not sure` verdict coaches the user to turn the box or **ask a pharmacist** ("or ask a pharmacist"), making the community pharmacist the mandatory human safety net.

## 2. Pre-Pilot Security and Privacy Gate (Closing docs/PRIVACY.md Section 6)

Before enrolling the first Phase 0 participant, every hackathon-build limitation documented in `docs/PRIVACY.md` section 6 is closed as a hard engineering release gate:

1. **Per-user device pairing token:** Replace unauthenticated `/hud-ws` latest-session attach, open `/ingest/frame` posting, and the shared demo `HOLDUP_KEY` (`server/src/auth.ts`) with a per-user cryptographic pairing token binding the participant's phone PWA (`web/src/lib/checker.ts`), Android DAT bridge (`android-dat/`), and Ray-Ban Display HUD Web App (`web/src/screens/Hud.tsx`, `/hud/`).
2. **Immediate session memory wipe on disconnect:** Drop the server's in-memory medication profile, dose log, `lastVerdict`, and 8-frame ring buffer (`server/src/verify.ts`, `server/src/hub.ts`) **immediately when the phone WebSocket disconnects**, replacing the **10 minutes** demo reconnect grace window in `server/src/hub.ts`, and add automated time-based TTL cleanup to the content-free metadata ledger (`server/src/ledger.ts`, currently 5 MB size-rotated).
3. **Paid Google Cloud / Gemini project with EU DPA:** Move from the hackathon API key (`docs/PRIVACY.md` section 5) to a **paid, billing-enabled Google Cloud / Gemini API project** governed by an **EU Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** so frames, audio, and prompts are never retained to train models, behind a dedicated TLS origin instead of an ad-hoc `cloudflared` quick tunnel.
4. **Native clinical translation sign-off:** Have native Finnish and Swedish pharmacists verify both the fixed deterministic openers in `server/src/live.ts` (`Oikea pakkaus.` / `Varoitus.` / `En ole varma.` and `Rätt förpackning.` / `Varning.` / `Jag är inte säker.`) and Gemini Live's translated detail tails, while keeping all existing `main` safeguards intact: three-card consent (`web/src/screens/Onboarding.tsx`), `45 s` camera idle auto-stop, hardware capture LED, bystander face-ignore rules (`server/src/prompts/live.md`, `shared/verify_prompt.md`), on-device `localStorage` profile (`web/src/lib/storage.ts`), and single-button `deleteAllData`.

## 3. Three-Channel Rollout Workflow

HoldUp enters daily life through the three support channels that blind and older Finns already trust (`deck/index.html` Slide 2 and Slide 12; full citations in `docs/RESEARCH.md`):

- **(a) Näkövammaisten liitto (NKL) peer-support and accessibility onboarding:** Finland has **`~55,000`** visually impaired people, **`~45,000`** of them aged **65+** (THL/NKL Näkövammarekisteri 2024; **2.2 billion** people with vision impairment globally, WHO 2026). In McCann et al. (*Br J Gen Pract* 2012, `n = 314`), **`97%`** (`152 / 156`) of older visually impaired adults struggled to read medication labels despite optical aids (sighted controls: `4%`), **`24%`** struggled to tell tablets apart, and they had **`2.8x`** the odds of needing help managing medication (`29% vs 13%`). NKL peer trainers onboard members onto TalkBack/VoiceOver, Bluetooth glasses audio, and voice-driven hold-up-to-add profile setup.
- **(b) Finnish pharmacy pickup:** **`21.6%`** ("1 in 5") of Finns aged **75+** bought **10+** different prescription medicines within four months in 2025 (Fimea / Kela Kanta Prescription Centre), while EU law (Directive 2001/83/EC Art. 56a) mandates Braille only for the product name, leaving the printed pharmacy dosing sticker and expiry unreadable by touch. At pickup, the pharmacist scans the pack's **EU FMD GS1 DataMatrix** (`server/src/gs1.ts`: GTIN + expiry) and adds the schedule into the user's on-device profile in **`< 30 s`**, then acts as the escalation point for every `Not sure` read.
- **(c) Wellbeing services counties (`hyvinvointialueet`) home-care nurses:** County home-care nurses verify the on-device profile and review the phone's `localStorage` **dose log** ("I took it") during scheduled visits, while HoldUp covers the independent morning and evening hours in between.

## 4. Hardware, Accessibility, and Unit Economics

- **Shipping hardware plus zero-install PWA fallback:** HoldUp is built for consumer Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses (Android DAT bridge APK compiled and unit-tested; today the full loop runs on the phone camera with the glasses as a Bluetooth headset while the on-glasses run is logged in docs/HARDWARE_LOG.md; bridge source in `android-dat/`, and blind users already wear Ray-Ban Meta with **Be My Eyes** across **`21 markets`**, `docs/RESEARCH.md`), **Ray-Ban Display** (`600x600` HUD Web App at `web/src/screens/Hud.tsx` / `/hud/` + Neural Band input, `docs/HARDWARE_NOTES.md`), and any smartphone browser as a zero-install **EN/FI/SV phone PWA fallback** (`web/`, `0` axe-core WCAG 2.2 A+AA violations across 6 audited screens in `docs/evidence/a11y-axe.json` plus Privacy and Profile tabs in `scripts/a11y-privacy-check.mjs`).
- **On-device spatial coaching and offline resilience:** Saved packs match offline by barcode (`web/src/lib/barcode.ts`), and our distilled **`12.8 MB`** (`12,776,109` bytes) on-device YOLOv8n **`fieldspotter.onnx`** (`web/public/models/fieldspotter.onnx`, `server/src/spotter.ts` behind `SPOTTER=1`) runs on CPU at **`34.5 ms`** p50 (`35.3 ms` p95, `96.8%` strength-visible precision and `65.6%` recall on `n = 348` held-out validation images; `docs/evidence/verda-fieldspotter.md`, `eval/results/fieldspotter.json`), paired with a Verda A100 **PaliGemma 2 3B LoRA** detector comparison (`GEMMA.md`, `eval/results/fieldspotter_gemma.json`: **`91.4%`** strength-visible recall and `89.9%` precision on `n = 348`, evaluated offline rather than wired into live coaching).
- **Adversarial safety red-team before pilot:** Alongside the `1,740`-image synthetic benchmark (`docs/EVAL.md`), the WS1 **32-family** safety red-team evaluated **`2,264`** adversarial images (`2,048` specs, `566` verified renders in `eval/results/redteam_v1.json`), cutting silent unsafe reassurance from `199 / 2,264 = 8.79%` to **`13 / 2,264 = 0.57%`** (Wilson 95% CI `0.34-0.98%`, `0` wrong-drug or wrong-strength matches; **in-sample** caveat because `server/src/safety.ts` guards and `199` fixtures in `server/test/safety-redteam-ws1.test.ts` were written from those failures, while held-out v2 stays `3 / 4,730 = 0.06%`).
- **Metered sub-cent unit economics:** Server telemetry in `/api/stats` (`docs/evidence/api-stats-snapshot.json`) shows direct `verify` on **`gemini-3.8-flash`** (`shared/verify_prompt.md`, `shared/label_schema.json`) used **`30,852`** input tokens, **`1,672`** output tokens, and **`1,071`** thinking tokens across **`12`** calls (**`2,571`** input + **`~229`** output+thinking tokens per call; speculative `verify_spec` averaged `~2,896` in + `~318` out+thinking across `49` calls). At standard Gemini Flash pricing each check is **sub-cent**, keeping HoldUp **free for users** while funded through pharmacy adherence services, `hyvinvointialueet` assistive-aid procurement, or accessibility glasses bundles (`deck/index.html` Slide 12).

## 5. Measurable Pilot KPIs and Stop Rules

| Metric | Committed repo baseline | Phase 0 pilot target (4 weeks, n = 20) | Hard stop / escalation rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Confident-wrong strength reads** (`confidence >= 0.8`) | **`0 / 754`** (`1,740` v1 images, 95% upper bound `0.4%`, `eval/results/v1_results.json`); **`1 / 536 = 0.19%`** on **`1,060`** held-out v3 images (`eval/results/v3_results.json`, `docs/EVAL.md`) | **`0`** confident-wrong strength reads | Pause cohort immediately and audit `server/src/safety.ts` + `shared/verify_prompt.md` on **`>= 1`** event |
| **Silent unsafe reassurance** (end-to-end) | **`3 / 4,730 = 0.06%`** (CI `0.02-0.19%`, down from `49 / 4,730 = 1.04%`, `eval/results/v2_safety_rescore.json`); **`4 / 3,033 = 0.13%`** held-out v3 (`14 / 4,041 = 0.35%` extended look-alikes, `eval/results/v3_heldout_safety.json`, `docs/EVAL.md`) | **`< 0.1%`** with EU FMD GS1 DataMatrix cross-check active on prescription packs | Pause affected food/expiry check path on **`>= 1`** silent unsafe reassurance on a real pack |
| **Question-to-first-audio latency** (`live`) | **`2.1 s` p50 / `3.4 s` p95** (`2.09 s` / `3.45 s` unrounded, **`n = 26`** live sessions; `docs/evidence/smoke-runs.md`, `docs/evidence/api-stats-snapshot.json`) | **`p50 < 2.5 s`**, `p95 < 4.0 s` over home Wi-Fi / 4G | Tune speculative cadence or fall back to `gemini-3.5-flash` (`2.0 s` p50 in `docs/EVAL.md`) if `p50 >= 2.5 s` |
| **Real-photo pre-gate & calibration** | Calibration ECE **`0.022`** (`0.018-0.028`); real Finnish pack set (`eval/real/`) pending separate score (`docs/EVAL.md`) | **`0`** confident-wrong strengths on >= 30 real Finnish pharmacy/grocery photos before week 1 | Block home deployment until `eval/real/` passes with `0` confident-wrong doses |
