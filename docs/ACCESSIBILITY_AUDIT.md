# HoldUp Accessibility Audit, WCAG 2.2 AA/AAA Conformance & Screen-Reader Guide (`docs/ACCESSIBILITY_AUDIT.md`)

HoldUp is built **screen-reader-first and multimodal-by-default** across Meta Ray-Ban Gen 2 open-ear audio, Meta Ray-Ban Display (`600x600` additive waveguide HUD + Neural Band sEMG gestures), and the React 19 companion PWA. Every contrast ratio, pixel target, acoustic earcon, keyboard shortcut, and ARIA live-region contract documented below is enforced by automated unit tests (`web/test/contrast.test.ts`, `web/test/a11y.test.ts`, `web/test/components-a11y.test.ts`, `web/test/earcons.test.ts`, `web/test/shortcutsModal.test.ts`, and `web/test/i18n.test.ts`).

## 3. Acoustic Earcons, Haptics & Keyboard/Gesture Reference

### 3.1 Synthesized Web Audio Earcon & Haptic Palette (`web/src/audio/earcons.ts`)
Zero external audio files are fetched over the network (ensuring `100%` offline reliability). Every state transition pairs a distinct frequency contour, oscillator timbre (`sine`, `triangle`, `square`), and tactile `navigator.vibrate` cadence:

| `EarconKind` | Frequencies (`Hz`) | Note Duration | Waveform | Gain | Haptic `vibratePattern` (`ms`) | Perceptual Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `camera_on` | `[440, 659.25]` *(A4 -> E5 rising fifth)* | `70 ms` | `sine` | `0.14` | `[30]` | Explicit confirmation that live camera capture has started |
| `camera_off` | `[659.25, 440]` *(E5 -> A4 falling fifth)* | `75 ms` | `sine` | `0.12` | `[20, 35, 20]` | Confirmation that camera stopped (manual or `45 s` idle shutoff) |
| `checking` | `[523.25]` *(C5 soft pulse every `1200 ms`)* | `45 ms` | `sine` | `0.05` | `[12]` | `verify_label` multi-frame extraction in progress (`Hold still`) |
| `cue` | `[698.46]` *(F5 crisp tap)* | `55 ms` | `triangle` | `0.10` | `[22]` | New body-relative aiming cue (`rotate_left`, `other_side`, `closer`) |
| `match` | `[523.25, 659.25, 783.99]` *(C5-E5-G5 major triad)* | `95 ms` | `triangle` | `0.18` | `[45, 50, 90]` | Verified safe prescription match (`STRENGTH_MIN_CONF >= 0.80`) |
| `warning` | `[830.61, 493.88]` *(G#5 -> B4 urgent tritone/sixth drop)* | `140 ms` | `square` | `0.16` | `[140, 70, 140, 70, 180]` | Contraindication (`NSAIDs`), allergen hit, wrong strength, expired, or duplicate dose |
| `unsure` | `[392, 392]` *(G4 double neutral tone)* | `80 ms` | `triangle` | `0.13` | `[40, 60, 40]` | Strength `< 0.80` or unreadable label; refuses to guess |
| `info` | `[587.33, 783.99]` *(D5 -> G5 rising fourth)* | `85 ms` | `sine` | `0.14` | `[50]` | Safe food package (`allergens >= 0.60`, zero profile hits) or general read |

### 3.2 Modifier-Free Keyboard & Neural Band Bindings (`web/src/a11y/shortcuts.ts` & `ShortcutsModal.tsx`)
- **`Space`**: Toggle active live label check ON/OFF (`toggle_check`)
- **`S`**: Capture and verify a single frame via HTTP Snapshot mode (`snapshot_verify`)
- **`B`**: Scan medication barcode locally offline against `localStorage` (`offline_barcode`)
- **`L`**: Log taken dose for a verified `match` (`log_dose`)
- **`R`**: Repeat the last spoken verdict or coaching cue (`repeat`)
- **`T`**: Cycle visual theme (`dark` -> `light` -> `high-contrast`, `cycle_theme`)
- **`P`**: Open Consent, Privacy & Local Data Controls (`open_privacy`)
- **Meta Neural Band / Ray-Ban Display**: Thumb swipe `ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight` moves focus along the 1D `/hud` action strip (`Repeat` default index `0`, `Log Dose` shown only on unlogged `match`, `Dismiss`); index pinch `Enter` activates (`150 ms` debounce); system Back pops the 2-entry history state back to `#000000` transparent standby (`docs/HUD_SPEC.md`).

## 4. Ten-Point VoiceOver (iOS) & TalkBack (Android) Rehearsal Checklist

Run this 10-point physical screen-reader pass before the Sunday `06:00` freeze (`ivarakerholm-wq` sign-off in `docs/SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md`):

1. - [ ] **Skip-link & landmark rotor navigation:** Swipe right from top-left (VoiceOver 1-finger flick / TalkBack swipe right, or external keyboard `Tab` #1): confirm `.skip-link` focuses visibly (`>= 56 px`), announces clearly, and moves focus directly into `<main>`, and confirm Headings/Landmarks rotor lists `Consent & On-Device Data Controls`, `Interactive judge demo walkthrough`, and `Label safety verdict`.
2. - [ ] **Trilingual Spoken Audio Orientation (`ShortcutsModal`):** Open `Keyboard & Gesture Guide`, activate **`Play Spoken Orientation`** (`Toista puhuttu opastus` in `fi` / `Spela upp rostguide` in `sv`), and confirm spoken synthesis + polite live status announcement (`Playing spoken orientation guide.`) complete cleanly without colliding with screen-reader speech, and `Escape` restores focus to the trigger button.
3. - [ ] **Consent Onboarding & Two-Step Data Wipe Safety (`ConsentOnboarding`):** Navigate the 5 numbered privacy commitment cards (`01` through `05`) by swipe; tap **`Delete All My Local Data`** and confirm `aria-live="assertive"` announces the confirmation prompt (`Confirmation required...`) *before* any deletion occurs; tap **`Cancel`** (`Delete cancelled. Your local data is unchanged.`), then tap **`Load Demo Profile (3 Meds + Nut Allergy)`** and verify polite confirmation.
4. - [ ] **Explicit Camera Start/Stop Earcons & Haptics:** Trigger a check via `Space` or the primary check button; verify the rising `camera_on` earcon (`440 -> 659.25 Hz` + `30 ms` vibration) fires immediately, the active camera status is announced, and stopping (or waiting `45 s` idle) plays the falling `camera_off` earcon (`659.25 -> 440 Hz`).
5. - [ ] **Repeated Coaching Cue Reliability (`ScreenReaderAnnouncer` Dual-Slot):** Trigger two identical consecutive coaching cues (e.g., `other_side`: *"Turn it around, the label is on the other side"* twice); confirm `polite-a` and `polite-b` alternate DOM text so VoiceOver/TalkBack speaks the second cue aloud instead of dropping it as an unchanged string.
