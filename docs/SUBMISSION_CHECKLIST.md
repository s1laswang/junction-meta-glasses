### Project Title & Tagline
- **Project Name:** HoldUp
- **Tagline:** Hold it up. Know it is the right one.
- **One-Line Subtitle:** Hands-free medication and allergen label assistant for blind, low-vision, and older adults on Meta AI Glasses, powered by Gemini Live.

### Short Pitch
HoldUp turns Meta AI Glasses into a hands-free medication and food-allergen verification assistant for blind, low-vision, and older adults. Hold a pill box or food carton naturally in both hands: Gemini Live coaches hand rotation, distance, and lighting at ~1 fps until the print is sharp, runs multi-frame schema-locked extraction on Gemini 3.8 Flash with per-field confidence, and checks the label deterministically against your on-device medication schedule, drug-class avoid rules, and allergies. It speaks a calm 2-sentence answer over open-ear audio, projects a high-contrast 4-channel card on Ray-Ban Display, and enforces a hard `0.80` strength-confidence gate so it never guesses a dose.

### Problem
Roughly 2.2 billion people worldwide live with vision impairment (around 80,000 in Finland, over 80% of whom are aged 65+), alongside older adults managing daily polypharmacy with declining sight. Several times every day, nearly identical white pharmacy cartons and fine-print food packages create a high-stakes question: *"Is this my evening Amlodipine 5 mg or Ibuprofen 400 mg? Has it expired? Are hazelnuts or peanut oils listed?"*

Today's smartphone workarounds break down in everyday life:
1. **The three-hand problem:** Rotating a curved bottle, steadying a cane, or holding a pill and a glass of water already takes two hands; holding and blind-aiming a phone camera requires a third.
2. **Single-shot OCR roulette:** Static snapshot apps read unprioritized raw text when the barcode or blank side faces the lens, give zero physical alignment guidance, and have zero awareness of what medications, doses, or allergies belong to the user.
3. **Loss of privacy and independence:** Video-calling a volunteer or asking a family member is slow, not always available at night or in a grocery aisle, and exposes private health routines.

### Solution
HoldUp pairs the chest-level first-person perspective of Meta AI Glasses with real-time multimodal coaching and deterministic personal safety checks:
1. **Ask naturally hands-free:** Trigger a check by voice (*"Is this my evening pill?"*, *"Onko tämä minun iltalääkkeeni?"*, or *"Does this contain nuts?"*), the glasses capture button, Meta Neural Band pinch, or single-key accessible controls.
2. **Active body-relative coaching (`gemini-3.8-live`):** Streaming ~1 fps first-person frames let Gemini Live speak concise physical cues (*"Turn it around"*, *"Tilt it away from the light"*, *"Move closer, hold still"*) while projecting directional glyphs on `/hud` via `show_cue`.
3. **Multi-frame schema-locked read (`gemini-3.8-flash`):** Once readable, `verify_label` selects the sharpest recent frames from a bounded 8-frame RAM ring buffer (`FrameRing`) and extracts structured `LabelRead` fields (`shared/label_schema.json`) with `[0, 1]` confidence per field.
4. **Deterministic personal safety engine (`server/src/safety.ts`):** Extracted fields are checked deterministically against the wearer's on-device `Profile` (`localStorage` only: medications, schedules, allergies, avoid-lists, and `doseLog`), resolving Nordic brand-to-INN mappings, drug classes (NSAIDs, anticoagulants, beta blockers, ACE inhibitors, penicillins, PPIs, antihistamines), EU-14 food allergens across English, Finnish, and Swedish, and expiry dates. If strength is missing or confidence is `< 0.80` (`STRENGTH_MIN_CONF = 0.80`), HoldUp refuses to guess and instructs the user to re-aim or ask a pharmacist.
5. **Multimodal confirmation, dose logging, caregiver alerts, and pharmacy onboarding:** Wearers hear a `<= 2`-sentence spoken verdict over private open-ear audio, see a `<= 8`-word 4-channel card (`MATCH`, `WARNING`, `UNSURE`, `INFO`) on Ray-Ban Display (`/hud`), log taken doses with double-dose protection, import prescriptions via Finnish pharmacy `holdup://rx` QR codes, and share 1-tap plain-text or SMS/email caregiver adherence summaries (`CaregiverAlertCard`).

### Meta "AI Glasses for Everyone, Everyday" Track Statement
HoldUp is built natively around why smart glasses are a fundamentally better everyday medium than a smartphone, not a phone screen mirrored onto a lens:
- **Proprioceptive hands-free POV:** A head-mounted camera naturally points at what two hands hold at chest height, freeing both hands to rotate packages, open blister packs, or hold a mobility cane.
- **Audio-first and display-first parity:** Blind wearers get a complete eyes-free loop via Bluetooth HFP open-ear audio, synthesized Web Audio earcons, haptics, and VoiceOver/TalkBack screen-reader support (`WCAG 2.2 AA` / `AAA` verified contrast tokens). Low-vision wearers additionally get an additive waveguide HUD on **Meta Ray-Ban Display** (`/hud`: pure `#000000` transparent canvas, `<= 65%` lit pixel fill budget to prevent waveguide bloom, `<= 8` word headline cap, and 4-channel shape + stroke icon + uppercase badge + high-luma color encoding) controlled by **Meta Neural Band** thumb swipes (`ArrowLeft`/`ArrowRight`) and index pinch (`Enter`) within a `<= 2`-entry browser history budget.

### Google DeepMind "Best Use of Gemini API" Prize Statement
HoldUp uses the Gemini API both as a **split-brain safety-critical runtime architecture** and as an **offline data and evaluation workforce**:
1. **Real-Time Multimodal Coaching & Grounded Tool Calling (`gemini-3.8-live`):** Streams 16 kHz PCM16 voice input, 24 kHz native audio output (`582 ms` measured first-audio latency, `706 ms` WS connect in `eval/live_models_probe.json`), and ~1 fps camera frames over `@google/genai` `v1beta` with barge-in interruption handling, `slidingWindow` context compression, `sessionResumption`, and three tools (`show_cue`, `verify_label`, `log_dose`). Crucially, `server/src/prompts/live.md` forbids the Live model from stating drug names, strengths, expiries, or allergens from its own video stream, eliminating conversational vision hallucinations.
2. **Low-Latency Schema-Constrained Verification (`gemini-3.8-flash`) & Multilingual TTS (`gemini-3.8-flash-tts`):** `readLabel()` (`server/src/verify.ts`) sends 1-3 sharpest frames with `ThinkingLevel.LOW`, `responseMimeType: "application/json"`, and `responseJsonSchema: LABEL_SCHEMA` (zero `$ref`s after inlining) while withholding the user's `Profile` so model extraction cannot be biased by expected prescriptions. Both Live and Verify paths enforce concurrency gates, `AbortSignal` timeouts, jittered backoff, and 3-model fallback chains (`gemini-3.8-live -> gemini-3.1-flash-live-preview -> gemini-2.5-flash-native-audio-latest`; `gemini-3.8-flash -> gemini-3.5-flash -> gemini-3-flash-preview`), plus `POST /api/tts` 24 kHz WAV fallback synthesis (`6/6` valid WAVs across `en`/`fi`/`sv` in `eval/tts_multilingual_audio.json`).
3. **Gemini Offline Workforce & 22-Artifact Safety Benchmark (`eval/*.json`):** `server/scripts/generate-demo-fixtures.ts` uses `gemini-3.1-flash-image` to synthesize photorealistic first-person pharmacy and food packages across 7 challenging scenarios, while `eval/judge_coaching.py` and our optical/clinical evaluation suites run real Gemini probes across `22` committed JSON artifacts ([`EVAL.md`](EVAL.md)): **`0` confident-wrong dose reads** (`STRENGTH_MIN_CONF = 0.80`) across all `200` evaluated label reads (`52/53` = `98.1%` core 4-suite verdict accuracy at `p50 = 3537.1 ms`, `ECE = 0.0138` / `Brier = 0.0005` on `16`-read calibration, `10/10` curved bottles & blister foil glare, `12/12` low-light & hand-tremor blur, `10/10` LASA polypharmacy & double-dose traps, `10/10` international/Nordic/debossed expiry formats, `12/12` 6-language European packages, `10/10` adversarial botanical/Nordic/excipient allergens, `4/4` multi-stage `FrameRing` coaching trajectories, `6/6` partial-view 2-frame recoveries, `3/3` live E2E verify-to-HUD trials at `p50 = 2738.8 ms`, and `100%` across all `3` Flash fallback models), **`0` false-safe allergen verdicts**, **`0` bystander/PII leaks across `8/8` (`100%`) red-team scenes** (`eval/bystander_privacy_results.json`), and **`8 / 8` (`100%`, mean `5.00 / 5`)** LLM-judged coaching/privacy/Finnish-switch checks (`eval/coaching_eval.json`, multi-turn `p50 = 5118.3 ms`, bystander refusal `2574.8 ms`).

### Technical Challenges & Resilience
1. **Bridging Meta Wearables hardware boundaries:** Ray-Ban Display Web Apps run in an on-glasses Chromium container without `getUserMedia` camera or raw microphone streams, while first-person camera access requires native Android/iOS DAT SDK `1.0.1`. We unified **one Node/TypeScript backend (`server/src/hub.ts`)** across **three thin clients** (Phone/Laptop React 19 PWA on `/live`, Android DAT bridge on `/ingest/frame` and `/ingest/state`, and Display HUD on `/hud-ws`, measured at `1.17 ms` ingest p50 and `1.78 ms` `/hud-ws` late-join replay p50 in `eval/hud_latency_bandwidth.json`). Session-less `floatingHuds` auto-bind to the latest active session and deterministically replay `status -> device -> coach -> verdict` so wearers never type pairing codes on glasses ([`ARCHITECTURE_DEEP_DIVE.md`](ARCHITECTURE_DEEP_DIVE.md)).
2. **Preventing confident dose hallucinations:** Single-model vision prompts can guess `5 mg` vs `50 mg` from box colors. We separated conversational coaching (`gemini-3.8-live`), blind schema-locked OCR (`gemini-3.8-flash` with `null` + `confidence <= 0.3` on ambiguous digits), and deterministic TypeScript verification (`STRENGTH_MIN_CONF = 0.80` in `server/src/safety.ts`), verified across `49` dedicated safety unit tests covering unit conversions (`mcg`/`mg`/`g`/`IU`/`KY`), combination drugs (`500 mg + 30 mg`), Nordic brands, and all EU-14 allergens, plus `200` real-model label evaluations with `0` confident-wrong doses.
3. **End-to-end fault tolerance under real-world mobility:** Tested by **`456` automated unit tests across `64` test files** (`node scripts/verify-all.mjs`, `node scripts/verify-submission.mjs`, and `node scripts/smoke-production.mjs`) and inspectable live via the in-app **Device & Resilience Panel** chaos toggles:
   - **Camera or mic permission denied:** Plain-language recovery steps (`PermissionBanner`); mic denial shifts cleanly to camera + typed question + TTS; camera denial preserves 7-scenario Demo Prop Mode.
   - **Live WebSocket drop:** Bounded exponential-backoff reconnect (`300 ms` to `8000 ms`) + Gemini `sessionResumption` while immediately routing checks through HTTP **Snapshot Mode** (`POST /api/verify` = `38.67 KB` + HUD fan-out + `/api/tts` or `speechSynthesis`).
   - **Complete offline loss:** Switches automatically to **Offline Mode**, matching EAN/UPC/QR codes locally via `BarcodeDetector` against saved `localStorage` `meds[].barcode` and `doseLog` with `0 KB` network usage, while stating clearly that new unsaved labels require connectivity.
   - **Slow venue Wi-Fi:** Continuous 5-second `ping`/`pong` RTT probing scales capture dynamically (`1.0 fps` @ `768 px` JPEG `0.78` under `250 ms` -> `0.75 fps` @ `640 px` JPEG `0.64` -> `0.5 fps` @ `512 px` JPEG `0.50` above `650 ms`, cutting video bandwidth by **`84.0%`** in `eval/unit_economics.json`).
   - **Glasses doffed, disconnected, or low battery:** Announces the transition aloud and hot-swaps active camera `source` from `"glasses"` to `"phone"` mid-session without losing profile or conversation state.
   - **Privacy by code enforcement:** Capture starts only on explicit user action, plays `camera_on`/`camera_off` earcons, auto-stops after `45 s` idle (`IDLE_STOP_MS = 45000`), clears the 8-frame RAM ring immediately on socket close (`hub.ring.clear()`), scrubs `DENY` keys and `AIza...` tokens in `server/src/log.ts`, keeps hardware LED locked ON via DAT firmware, refuses bystander descriptions (`8/8` pass in both `eval/coaching_eval.json` and `eval/bystander_privacy_results.json`), and wipes all `holdup.*` local data and saved Android bridge endpoints in one tap (`wipeAllLocalData()`, `BridgePreferences.clearSavedEndpoint`).
