### 3.2 `leadFor()` and `speakInstruction()` hardening (`server/src/live.ts:83-92`)

1. **Case-insensitive locale normalization:** Previously `(lang || "en").slice(0, 2)` returned `undefined` for uppercase `"FI"` or `"SV"`. Fixed via `normLangCode(lang) = (lang || "en").trim().toLowerCase().slice(0, 2)`.
2. **Preventing double-opener stutter (`"Varoitus. Varoitus: ..."`):** Because `safety.ts` starts English match/warning sentences with `"Yes, ..."` or `"Warning: ..."`, `speakInstruction()` previously told Gemini Live to say `lead` (`"Varoitus."`) and then translate `speech` (`"Warning: ..."`), producing a stuttered `"Varoitus. Varoitus: tämä on..."`. `speakInstruction()` now explicitly instructs: `do not repeat the opener`.
3. **Preventing the Finnish dot-decimal date trap (`2.5 mg` -> `"May 2nd"`):** `shared/verify_prompt.md:15` normalizes strengths to dot decimals (`"2,5 mg"` -> `"2.5 mg"`). In Finnish orthography, `2.5.` is the ordinal date **2nd of May** (*toinen toukokuuta*). If Gemini Live or a TTS engine leaves `"2.5 mg"` with a period in Finnish/Swedish output, TTS can read it as a date or `"kaksi piste viisi"`. `speakInstruction()` now explicitly requires: `using a decimal comma like 2,5 mg when speaking numbers`.

## 4. Finnish & Swedish allergen, trace-warning, and drug-class grammar (`server/src/safety.ts`)

### 4.1 Why nominative-only lists fail on real Nordic food and medicine packs

In `server/src/safety.ts:213-215`, `hasWord(hay, needle)` enforces exact non-letter boundaries (`(^|[^a-z])needle([^a-z]|$)`).
- **Finnish partitive & genitive suffixes:** After `"Sisältää"` (contains), `"Voi sisältää"` / `"Saattaa sisältää"` (may contain), or in ingredient lists, Finnish nouns take the **partitive singular** (`-a/-ä/-ta/-tä`, e.g., `maito` -> `maitoa`, `vehnä` -> `vehnää`, `kananmuna` -> `kananmunaa`, `maapähkinä` -> `maapähkinää`, `hasselpähkinä` -> `hasselpähkinää`, `manteli` -> `mantelia`, `soija` -> `soijaa`, `kala` -> `kalaa`, `sinappi` -> `sinappia`, `selleri` -> `selleriä`) or **genitive** before `"jäämiä"` (`pähkinä` -> `pähkinän jäämiä`, `maapähkinän jäämiä`). Because `hasWord("voi sisaltaa maitoa", "maito")` is **`false`**, a partitive label term extracted verbatim by `verify_label` previously missed `allergenHits()`!
- **Swedish plural & compound forms:** Swedish trace warnings use the **indefinite plural** (`"Kan innehålla spår av jordnötter / hasselnötter / mandlar / valnötter / cashewnötter / kräftdjur / blötdjur"`). Because `hasWord("kan innehalla jordnotter", "jordnot")` is **`false`**, plural Swedish nut terms previously missed `allergenHits()`!
- **Missing EU Regulation 1169/2011 Annex II terms:** Group 14 (`molluscs`) previously had only English `"molluscs"` (`0` Finnish or Swedish terms), `crustacean` lacked Swedish `"kräftdjur"` / `"skaldjur"` / `"räkor"`, and `sulphite` lacked Swedish `"svaveldioxid"` / `"sulfiter"`.

### 4.2 Fixes applied in `server/src/safety.ts`

1. **All 14 EU allergen groups (`ALLERGENS`, `safety.ts:65-150`)** now include Finnish nominative, partitive, genitive, plural, and compound forms (`maapahkinaa`, `maapahkinan`, `hasselpahkinaa`, `mantelia`, `pahkinan`, `maitoa`, `maidon`, `laktoosia`, `heraa`, `vehnaa`, `ruista`, `ohraa`, `gluteenia`, `kananmunaa`, `soijaa`, `seesamia`, `seesaminsiemenia`, `kalaa`, `lohta`, `ayriaisia`, `katkarapuja`, `nilviaiset`, `nilviaisia`, `simpukoita`, `sinappia`, `selleria`, `lupiinia`, `rikkidioksidia`, `sulfiitteja`) and Swedish indefinite/plural/compound forms (`jordnotter`, `hasselnotter`, `mandlar`, `valnotter`, `cashewnotter`, `notter`, `laktos`, `vassle`, `vetemjol`, `vetestarkelse`, `kornmalt`, `ragmjol`, `sojabonor`, `sojalecitin`, `sesamfron`, `kraftdjur`, `skaldjur`, `rakor`, `blotdjur`, `musslor`, `senapsfron`, `svaveldioxid`, `sulfiter`).
2. **Trace-warning regex (`safety.ts:425`)** expanded from `/may contain|voi sisaltaa|kan innehalla|traces?/` to `/may contain|voi sisaltaa|saattaa sisaltaa|jaamia|kan innehalla|spar av|traces?/`, catching Finnish `"Saattaa sisältää..."` and `"...jäämiä"` and Swedish `"spår av..."`.
3. **Profile allergy & avoid-list aliases (`safety.ts:56-63,152-162`)** expanded to accept Finnish/Swedish suffixes (`"pähkinäallergia"`, `"maitoallergia"`, `"keliakia"`, `"laktosintolerans"`, `"tulehduskipulääkkeet"`, `"inflammationshämmande"`, `"bentsodiatsepiinit"`, `"bensodiazepiner"`, `"statiinit"`, `"statiner"`).

## 5. Web PWA & Ray-Ban Display HUD strings (`web/src/`)

### 5.1 Fixed runtime bug in `web/src/lib/phrases.ts`

Before this PR:
1. `playPhrase(id, lang)` looked up `m?.phrases[lang]?.[id] ?? m?.phrases.en?.[id]`. If `lang` was `"fi-FI"` or if a Finnish MP3 was missing, it silently played the **English** MP3 to a Finnish user.
2. When `playPhrase()` returned `false` (no MP3 cached or autoplay blocked), `sayPhrase()` called `speak(fallbackText)` where `fallbackText` in `checker.ts` was hardcoded **English** (`"You're offline..."`), while `feedback.ts:87,107` set `SpeechSynthesisUtterance.lang` to `"fi-FI"` or `"sv-SE"`, causing a Finnish/Swedish voice to read English words phonetically.

**Fix (`web/src/lib/phrases.ts:8-70`):** `phrases.ts` now normalizes `lang` (`"fi-FI"` -> `"fi"`), never falls back to an English MP3 when `code !== "en"`, and exports `PHRASE_TEXT` + `phraseText(id, lang, fallbackText)` so `sayPhrase()` always speaks the native Finnish or Swedish phrase when MP3 playback is unavailable.
