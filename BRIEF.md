# Meta: AI Glasses for Everyone, Everyday (Junction 2026)
Page: https://hackjunction.app/participate/45/challenges/342. Deadline: Sun 15 Nov 2026 10:00 Helsinki.

**Challenge.** Build the best everyday use case for Meta AI glasses. Show how a first-person view enables new or better everyday interactions that go beyond mirroring a phone. It should be inclusive for people of all abilities. Display-first and audio-first entries count equally, and so do web apps and Device Access Toolkit (DAT) mobile integrations.

**Deliverables.**
- A functional prototype and a deck.
- A clear problem and audience.
- A plausible path to adoption or a pilot.
- The prototype must handle permissions, errors, performance trade-offs, connectivity, and device state changes.
- It must show consent and safe data practices.

**Judging (25% each).**
- Creativity and use case
- Technical execution
- Real-world value and feasibility
- UX: input, accessibility and trust

**Prize.** Each member of the winning team gets Ray-Ban Meta glasses. The best teams may be invited to Meta's AI forum on 18 Nov.

**Resources at the Meta booth.**
- 10 Meta Ray-Ban Display and 10 RBM Gen 2, shared, for use during the event.
- Mentors: Web Apps (Ming Lin, William La, Amy Lee, Odil Nikadambaev), DAT iOS (Dennis Punjabi), DAT Android and point of contact (Amanda Le).
