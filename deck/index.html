How HoldUp Works: The 5-Step Safety Loop

Live streaming AI handles physical hand coaching; schema-locked extraction + deterministic TypeScript rules govern every verdict.

1 · Explicit Trigger

Starts only on user voice, capture button, or Neural Band tap. Auto-stops after 45 s idle.

2 · Raw-Vision Ban

live.md forbids stating drug names or doses from Live video; Live only coaches hand pose until crisp.

3 · FrameRing(8) Pick

Picks sharpest frames in RAM (0 KB phone re-upload); normalizes 2,5 mg -> 2.5 mg; clamps null conf <= 0.30.

4 · Local Profile Gate

Cross-checks INN synonyms (Burana -> ibuprofen -> NSAID), EU-14 allergens, ISO expiry, and today's doseLog.

5 · Close the Loop

Relays speech verbatim, renders 4-channel HUD card, locks duplicate doses, and formats plain-text caregiver alerts.

HoldUp splits perception from safety rules across five steps. Gemini 3.8 Live is forbidden from reading doses off raw video: it coaches hand position and calls verify_label, which sends the sharpest frames from our RAM ring buffer to Gemini 3.8 Flash with a strict JSON schema, before deterministic TypeScript checks your local profile.
